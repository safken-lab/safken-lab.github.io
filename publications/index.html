  • Variational Inference for Uncertain Optimal Transport via Sinkhorn Parametrization

    Ananyapam De, Linus Bleistein, Anton Frederik Thielmann, Benjamin Säfken

    ICML · 2026

    Abstract summary

    Standard optimal transport produces a single plan for a fixed cost. This paper uses the Sinkhorn map to turn variational samples into transport plans that satisfy the marginal constraints. The method uses normalizing flows to estimate uncertainty over plans and achieves accuracy comparable to sampling approaches at lower computational cost in the reported experiments.

  • Beyond Black-Box Predictions: Identifying Marginal Feature Effects in Tabular Transformer Networks

    Anton Frederik Thielmann, Arik Reuter, Benjamin Säfken

    AISTATS · 2026

    Abstract summary

    The authors adapt a tabular transformer to identify marginal feature effects while retaining its ability to model interactions. They justify the approach theoretically and use ablation studies to test the recovery of feature effects. In the reported benchmarks, the model can match black-box predictors while exposing these effects.

  • From Uniform to Learned Knots: A Study of Spline-Based Numerical Encodings for Tabular Deep Learning

    Manish Kumar, Anton Frederik Thielmann, Christoph Weisser, Benjamin Säfken

    arXiv preprint · 2026

    Abstract summary

    The study compares B-splines, M-splines, and integrated splines under several knot-placement schemes, including jointly learned knots. Results depend on the task and model backbone. Piecewise-linear encoding is robust for classification, while regression performance varies by spline family and knot strategy. Learned knots can increase training cost.

  • EviNAM: Intelligibility and Uncertainty via Evidential Neural Additive Models

    Sören Schleibaum, Anton Frederik Thielmann, Julian Teusch, Benjamin Säfken, Jörg P. Müller

    arXiv preprint · 2026

    Abstract summary

    EviNAM combines evidential learning with neural additive models. A single forward pass estimates uncertainty from the data and uncertainty in the model while exposing feature contributions. The additive structure lets readers inspect the effects of individual predictors alongside uncertainty estimates.

  • Quality Without Usefulness: LLM-Generated XAI Narratives as Trust Heuristics Rather Than Decision Aids

    Fabian Lukassen, Jan Herrmann, Christoph Weisser, Alexander Silbersdorff, Benjamin Säfken, Thomas Kneib

    arXiv preprint · 2026

    Abstract summary

    This study tests whether natural-language explanations produced from explainable AI outputs actually help people make decisions. It examines how explanation quality, trust, and task performance relate. Its findings suggest that polished narratives can influence perceived trust even when they provide limited decision support.

  • LLM-Augmented Changepoint Detection: A Framework for Ensemble Detection and Automated Explanation

    Fabian Lukassen, Christoph Weisser, Michael Schlee, Manish Kumar, Anton Thielmann, Benjamin Säfken, Thomas Kneib

    arXiv preprint · 2026

    Abstract summary

    The paper combines an ensemble of statistical changepoint detectors with a language model. The ensemble pools evidence from methods with different strengths. The language model then generates explanations for the detected regime changes, addressing both detection and interpretation in a single workflow.

  • From XAI to Stories: A Factorial Study of LLM-Generated Explanation Quality

    Fabian Lukassen, Jan Herrmann, Christoph Weisser, Benjamin Säfken, Thomas Kneib

    arXiv preprint · 2026

    Abstract summary

    A factorial experiment varies the forecasting model, attribution method, language model, and prompting strategy used to turn numerical feature attributions into explanations. The analysis examines which choices affect the perceived quality of the resulting narratives and informs the design of explanation pipelines.

  • Penalized regression splines in Mixture Density Networks

    Quentin Edward Seifert, Anton Thielmann, Elisabeth Bergherr, Benjamin Säfken, Jakob Zierk, Manfred Rauh, Tobias Hepp

    The International Journal of Biostatistics · 2025

    Abstract summary

    The paper incorporates penalized regression splines into mixture density networks to model how an entire conditional distribution changes with predictors. Smoothing penalties control the flexibility of the fitted functions within the mixture model.

2024

  • NODE-GAMLSS: Interpretable Uncertainty Modelling via Deep Distributional Regression

    Ananyapam De, Anton Frederik Thielmann, Benjamin Säfken

    NeurIPS Workshop on Bayesian Decision-making and Uncertainty · 2024

    Abstract summary

    NODE-GAMLSS uses additive ensembles of differentiable decision trees to estimate how features affect the location, scale, and shape of a response distribution. The model supports quantile prediction and inspection of individual feature effects. The authors benchmark it against other interpretable distributional models on simulated and real data.

  • Neural Additive Models for Location Scale and Shape: A Framework for Interpretable Neural Regression Beyond the Mean

    Anton Frederik Thielmann, René-Marcel Kruse, Thomas Kneib, Benjamin Säfken

    AISTATS · 2024

    Abstract summary

    Neural Additive Models for Location, Scale and Shape extend interpretable additive networks beyond mean prediction. The model assigns additive feature effects to parameters of a response distribution, allowing users to inspect how predictors affect its location, spread, and shape.

  • Mambular: A Sequential Model for Tabular Deep Learning

    Anton Frederik Thielmann, Manish Kumar, Christoph Weisser, Arik Reuter, Benjamin Säfken, Soheila Samiee

    arXiv preprint · 2024

    Abstract summary

    Mambular treats the columns of tabular data as a sequence and processes them using structured state-space layers. The paper compares pooling strategies, feature interaction mechanisms, and bidirectional variants against tabular baselines. The results show that state-space models can be competitive for tabular prediction.

  • STREAM: Simplified Topic Retrieval, Exploration, and Analysis Module

    Anton Frederik Thielmann, Arik Reuter, Benjamin Säfken, Christoph Weisser, Manish Kumar, Gillian Kant

    ACL · 2024

    Abstract summary

    STREAM provides tools to fit, evaluate, explore, and visualize topic models. The paper introduces evaluation measures based on identifying intruder words and makes additional datasets available for benchmarking. Its interactive tools are intended to help users interpret topics in large text collections.

  • Probabilistic Topic Modelling with Transformer Representations

    Arik Reuter, Anton Thielmann, Christoph Weisser, Benjamin Säfken, Thomas Kneib

    arXiv preprint · 2024

    Abstract summary

    This paper brings probabilistic modelling to topics discovered in transformer embedding spaces. It models the geometry of contextual representations to recover topic distributions and uses the resulting probabilistic representation to characterize documents and their topics.

  • GPTopic: Dynamic and Interactive Topic Representations

    Arik Reuter, Bishnu Khadka, Anton Thielmann, Christoph Weisser, Sebastian Fischer, Benjamin Säfken

    arXiv preprint · 2024

    Abstract summary

    GPTopic uses language models to create topic descriptions and supports iterative interaction with a topic model. It lets users examine and refine how topics are described, making the output easier to interpret than lists of representative words alone.

  • Rage Against the Mean – A Review of Distributional Regression Approaches

    Thomas Kneib, Alexander Silbersdorff, Benjamin Säfken

    Econometrics and Statistics · 2023

    Abstract summary

    This review surveys distributional regression, where covariates can affect several aspects of a response distribution. It compares approaches to modelling location, scale, shape, and other distributional properties, and discusses when these approaches are useful in applications.

  • Conditional Model Selection in Mixed-Effects Models with cAIC4

    Benjamin Säfken, David Rügamer, Thomas Kneib, Sonja Greven

    Journal of Statistical Software · 2021

    Abstract summary

    The cAIC4 R package implements conditional Akaike information criteria for fitted mixed-effects models. The criteria account for uncertainty in estimated variance components. The package also provides stepwise model selection and the article demonstrates its use with practical examples.

  • Smoothing Parameter and Model Selection for General Smooth Models

    Simon N. Wood, Natalya Pya, Benjamin Säfken

    Journal of the American Statistical Association · 2016

    Abstract summary

    The authors develop a likelihood-based framework for selecting smoothing parameters in models with smooth covariate effects. Laplace approximate marginal likelihood estimates the penalties, and accounting for their uncertainty yields an improved model selection criterion. The framework covers a range of additive, distributional, and survival models.

  • A unifying approach to the estimation of the conditional Akaike information in generalized linear mixed models

    Benjamin Säfken, Thomas Kneib, Clara-Sophie van Waveren, Sonja Greven

    Electronic Journal of Statistics · 2014

    Abstract summary

    This paper develops conditional Akaike information criteria for generalized linear mixed models. Its unified formulation accounts for uncertainty in random effects and their covariance parameters. The results extend conditional model selection beyond Gaussian mixed models to several other response distributions.
